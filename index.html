🌿 About Sirat-ul-Quran

Sirat-ul-Quran is an educational platform created to make Quran learning simple, organized and accessible for beginners. Our aim is to provide structured learning resources and encourage students to build a meaningful connection with the Quran.


Students can explore Quran reading, basic Tajweed, Surah understanding and regular learning activities.
